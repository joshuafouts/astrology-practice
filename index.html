About

Joshua Fouts

I am a depth psychology therapist trained at Pacifica Graduate Institute in Santa Barbara, California — a tradition rooted in the work of Jung, Hillman, and the archetypal imagination.

Based in Helsinki, Finland, I work as a practicing counseling astrologer offering sessions both in person and online, in English and Portuguese.

I have been a practicing astrologer for over 40 years, and a member of the Indigenous plant medicine community across multiple traditions for nearly 20 years. These long relationships with both cosmic and sacred plant wisdom inform my approach and give me a deep understanding of the integration process.

I am honored to provide psychedelic integration counseling and coaching — helping people make meaning of their journeys and weave those insights into a life of greater authenticity and wholeness.
